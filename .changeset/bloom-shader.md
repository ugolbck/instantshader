New "Bloom" shader: an analytic fan of soft petals radiating from the frame's
bottom edge, with the palette laid out radially (hot core to dark background).
Two blendable motion modes via params: `sway` (petals breathe and lean) and
`colorflow` (colors travel outward through a static pattern).
